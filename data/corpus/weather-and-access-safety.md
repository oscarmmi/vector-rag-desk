# Weather and unsafe access decisions

Drivers may pause a stop when severe weather, blocked roads, unsafe animals, damaged steps, or threatening behavior makes access unsafe. Move to a safe location, notify dispatch, and document the condition with a neutral note. Do not enter private property beyond the authorized delivery point or attempt a risky workaround to protect a time-window metric. Dispatch decides whether to reroute, retry later, or return the parcel. Safety decisions take priority over route completion targets and must not be penalized.
