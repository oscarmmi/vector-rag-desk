# End-of-route closeout

At the end of a route, drivers reconcile delivered, returned, and handed-off parcels against the manifest. Every remaining parcel needs an exception code and a next action before the route can close. Return undelivered parcels to the marked intake area and scan them into depot custody with a team member present. The driver submits the final route report, flags vehicle or equipment issues, and confirms that customer callbacks assigned during the shift have an owner. Dispatch checks unresolved exceptions at the start of the next operating day.
