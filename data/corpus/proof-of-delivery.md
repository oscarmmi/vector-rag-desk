# Safe arrival and proof of delivery

Before completing a stop, confirm the address and follow the delivery instruction recorded for that order. Obtain the recipient's name or approved drop-off confirmation and record proof in the route app. Leave a parcel only in a location the recipient has authorized and that is protected from weather and public access. If the instruction cannot be followed safely, keep the parcel and record an exception. Photos must show the parcel at the authorized location without capturing unrelated people or sensitive details.
