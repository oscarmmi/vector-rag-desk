# Parcel loading and scan checks

Load parcels in reverse stop order so the first delivery is reachable without moving other freight. Scan every parcel onto the assigned route and compare the app count with the physical count before departure. Separate fragile items from heavy parcels, secure liquids upright, and keep restricted or temperature-sensitive items in their designated area. If a label is unreadable or a parcel is missing, pause release and ask the depot lead to correct the manifest. Never scan a parcel onto a route that does not have physical custody of it.
