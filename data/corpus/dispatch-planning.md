# Dispatch planning and route release

The dispatch lead reviews the next-day order queue at 17:00 and groups stops by service area, promised time window, vehicle capacity, and parcel handling needs. Routes are released only after every stop has a valid address, access notes, and a contact number. Keep a 15-minute buffer between dense urban clusters and never assign more stops than the driver can safely complete within the shift. Drivers receive the manifest in the route app before leaving the depot. The lead records late additions in the change log and tells the affected driver directly.
